Investors

Investor overview

Accessibility, a trusted community, and real professionals, with AI as the enabling layer, not the product.

Where we are today (early stage): the HFB brand, this website, our positioning and pitch materials, and our first live prototype, Choice & Reward. The platform described below is planned, not built.

Vision

Help neurodivergent people turn awareness into action and growth, with privacy-first tools grounded in science.

Problem

Families, professionals, and neurodivergent adults lack calm, trustworthy tools. Most options are either too clinical or too noisy, and language barriers make it harder.

Approach

AI alone is not the moat. A trusted community, accessibility, structured knowledge, and real professionals together are the differentiated system.

Why now

Awareness is rising, and AI makes translation, read-aloud, and simpler language practical. Human connection matters more in the AI era.

Six-month plan

These are planning targets, not completed milestones.

  1. Months 1–2

    MVP build, data foundation, first UI, translation and Read-Aloud.

  2. Month 3

    Small pilot with parents and professionals, community spaces, safety layer, accessibility refinement.

  3. Month 4

    Beta release and limited Expert Connect testing.

  4. Month 5

    Grow the Knowledge Hub, gather structured feedback, and carefully test early expert pathways.

  5. Month 6

    Update pitch materials, prepare wider outreach, and plan native mobile expansion.

Business model ideas

  • Early: expert Q&A, workshops, and coaching sessions, where legally appropriate
  • Later: subscriptions, premium accessibility tools, and school and clinic partnerships
  • Never: selling sensitive user data
