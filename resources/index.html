Autism & ADHD basics

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (CDC) (external site)Signs, screening and diagnosis, treatment, and data from the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.cdc.gov
  • ADHD (CDC) (external site)Symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, and tips for families from the CDC.cdc.gov
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (NIMH) (external site)A research-based overview from the US National Institute of Mental Health.nimh.nih.gov
  • ADHD (NIMH) (external site)Signs, causes and treatments of ADHD in children and adults.nimh.nih.gov
  • Autism fact sheet (WHO) (external site)Key facts about autism around the world from the World Health Organization.who.int
  • National Autistic Society (UK) (external site)Clear guides for autistic people, families and professionals from a UK charity.autism.org.uk

For families & caregivers

  • Learn the Signs. Act Early. (CDC) (external site)Free milestone checklists and the Milestone Tracker app, with tips for talking to your child’s doctor.cdc.gov
  • Autism on HealthyChildren.org (AAP) (external site)Information for parents from the American Academy of Pediatrics.healthychildren.org
  • Autism Society of America (external site)Information, referrals and local affiliates across the United States.autismsociety.org
  • CHADD (external site)A US nonprofit for people with ADHD and their families, including the National Resource Center on ADHD.chadd.org
  • Understood.org (external site)Practical help for learning and thinking differences such as ADHD and dyslexia, at home and at school.understood.org
  • Center for Parent Information and Resources (external site)Find your local US Parent Center for help with special education and your child’s rights at school.parentcenterhub.org

For neurodivergent adults

  • Autistic Self Advocacy Network (ASAN) (external site)A nonprofit run by and for autistic people, with self-advocacy and plain-language resources.autisticadvocacy.org
  • Attention Deficit Disorder Association (ADDA) (external site)Support, webinars and peer groups for adults with ADHD.add.org
  • AASPIRE Healthcare Toolkit (external site)Tools for autistic adults and their healthcare providers, including a healthcare accommodations report.researchautism.org
  • Job Accommodation Network (JAN) (external site)Free, confidential guidance on workplace accommodations from a US Department of Labor service.askjan.org

  • NICE guideline CG142 (external site)Autism spectrum disorder in adults: diagnosis and management (UK).nice.org.uk

  • Augmentative and Alternative Communication (ASHA) (external site)An introduction to AAC from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association.asha.org

If you need help now

If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.

  • In the United States, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7.
  • In other countries, Find A Helpline lists free, confidential helplines.

HFB is not a crisis service and cannot respond to emergencies.
