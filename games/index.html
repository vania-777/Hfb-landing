Calm, bilingual (Persian and English) games for an adult and a child to play side by side. They run in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no account and no download.

  • Recorded read-aloud in Persian and English

Match It: What goes together?

The child drags the item that goes with a picture: water to a glass, a key to a lock. Each match plays a small animation.

  • Drag with a finger or a mouse, or tap and tap
  • 12 hand-drawn pairs, each with its own little animation
  • A wrong match simply slides back. No red, no X

A surprise egg every 5 stars

In every game, five stars bring a big egg that wobbles, cracks, and opens to show a surprise: a little car that drives off, a dinosaur, a duck, a rocket, a butterfly, and more. Each one has its own sound and an excited line read aloud in Persian or English.

  • 12 surprises in a random order, never the same one twice in a row
  • Soft colors and no flashing. Tap to continue
  • You can turn it off in the game’s Adult panel

Tips for playing together

  • Keep it short

    A few minutes is plenty. Stop while it is still fun, and come back another time.

  • Sit side by side

    Point, wait, and let the child choose. Say the words out loud together.

  • Change the level

    Each game has levels in the Adult panel (the gear button). Go back a level whenever you like.

  • Make it calm

    Turn sound or animation off in the Adult panel if they feel like too much.

  • Use both languages

    Switch between Persian and English with the FA/EN button at any time.

  • For professionals

    Levels, prompts and repetition rules are explained on the game details page.

These games are prototypes. They are not clinical tools and have not been clinically evaluated.
