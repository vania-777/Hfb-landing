Play Together

Choice & Reward

A calm, bilingual Persian/English choice-making game for an adult (a caregiver, parent, or behavior technician) and a child to play together.

Open Choice & Reward

Choice & Reward

Play Together

🍎🧸

⭐⭐⭐☆☆

How Choice & Reward works

  1. The child sees 2, 3, or 4 large picture cards: food, toys, or activities.
  2. They pick one, by touch, keyboard, or by pointing while the adult taps.
  3. The game says “You chose …!” and adds a star.
  4. Five stars open a gentle celebration screen.

Made to be calm

  • Persian (right-to-left) and English
  • Read-aloud using your browser’s built-in voices, where available
  • Dark, dim, and light themes
  • Gentle motion, off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion
  • An adult panel for the number of choices, categories, sound, and motion
  • Play remotely by sharing your screen on a video call

Privacy: no account and no tracking. Settings and stars are saved only in your browser’s local storage.

Prototype. It is not a clinical tool.

Play Together

Feelings

A calm, bilingual Persian/English emotion-recognition game for an adult (a caregiver, parent, or behavior technician) and a child to play together, in person or over a video call.

Open Feelings

Feelings

Play Together

😊😢

⭐⭐☆☆☆

How Feelings works

  1. The child sees one friendly cartoon face and hears “How does this face feel?”
  2. They pick a feeling word from 2, 3, or 4 buttons. Tapping the face says the feeling’s name.
  3. A right answer plays a soft chime, says “Yes! This face is happy.”, and adds a star.
  4. A wrong answer gets no buzzer and no red X. The right answer is gently shown and named, and the game moves on.
  5. Five stars open a gentle celebration screen.

Made for practice

  • Happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, and calm, plus optional tired and disgusted
  • Hand-drawn cartoon faces with clear features and varied skin tones, no photos
  • An errorless-learning mode where the right answer gently pulses
  • Recorded read-aloud in Persian and English that also works on iPhone
  • An adult panel for choices, feelings, prompting, sound, motion, and stars
  • Play remotely by sharing your screen on a video call

Play Together

Match It: What goes together?

A calm, bilingual Persian/English drag-to-match game for an adult (a caregiver, parent, or behavior technician) and a child to play together, in person or over a video call.

Open Match It

Match It

Play Together

🔒🔑

⭐⭐⭐☆☆

How Match It works

  1. The child sees a picture, such as a glass, and hears “What goes with the glass?”
  2. They drag the matching card (water) onto the picture. A finger, a mouse, or tap-then-tap all work.
  3. A right match plays a short animation with a sound: the water pours and the glass fills. Then a star and praise: “Yes! Water goes in the glass!”
  4. A wrong card slides gently back with a soft sound, with no red and no X. After two tries, the right card gently wiggles.
  5. Five stars open the next level, or you can repeat the level.

Made for practice

  • 12 hand-drawn pairs: glass and water, lock and key, envelope and letter, foot and sock, toothbrush and toothpaste, paper and pencil, flower and bee, dog and bone, plant and watering can, head and hat, basket and ball, toy car and battery
  • Three levels with 2, 3, or 4 cards
  • Random order and card positions, and missed pairs come back a few rounds later
  • Recorded read-aloud in Persian and English; sound effects made in the browser
  • Animation can be turned off, and it is off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion
