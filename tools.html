Choice & Reward

Choice & Reward

Play Together

🍎🧸

⭐⭐⭐☆☆

How it works

  1. The child sees 2, 3, or 4 large picture cards: food, toys, or activities.
  2. They pick one, by touch, keyboard, or by pointing while the adult taps.
  3. The game says “You chose …!” and adds a star.
  4. Five stars open a gentle celebration screen.

Made to be calm

  • Persian (right-to-left) and English
  • Read-aloud using your browser’s built-in voices, where available
  • Dark, dim, and light themes
  • Gentle motion, off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion
  • An adult panel for the number of choices, categories, sound, and motion
  • Play remotely by sharing your screen on a video call

Privacy: no account and no tracking. Settings and stars are saved only in your browser’s local storage.

Prototype. It is not a clinical tool.
