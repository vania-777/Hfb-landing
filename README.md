# highfunctioningbrains.com

Static site for High Functioning Brains (HFB), hosted on GitHub Pages. No build step.

## Structure (October 2026)
- `index.html`: **Welcome** page for first-time visitors. Shows the brand and symbol, the "What brings you here?" choice (Families & caregivers, Therapists & educators, Neurodivergent adults, Investors & partners), **Enter HFB** (goes to `/home/`) and **Try a game**. Returning visitors who already chose a view skip it when they arrive from outside the site. The logo and "Back to the welcome page" always show it.
- `home/`: **Home hub**. Shows a "Picked for you" band for the chosen view, plus cards for every section and the games.
- `games/`: Games & Play
- `resources/`: Resources & Links, all from external reputable organizations
- `community/`: Community (coming soon) with a mailto waitlist. There is no chat backend
- `research/`: Research & Professional, with numbered citations (DOIs checked against Crossref)
- `wellbeing/`: Hope & Wellbeing
- `about.html`, `investors.html`, `product.html` (Platform plans), `tools.html` (game details), `docs.html`, `press-kit.html`, `privacy.html`, `404.html`
- `investor.html`, `build.html`, `philosophy.html`, `routines.html`, `docs-eight-leagues.html` are redirect stubs for old links
- `assets/site.css` is the only stylesheet. `assets/site.js` handles three things:
  - the theme (dark → dim → light, saved in localStorage `hfb-theme`)
  - the mobile menu
  - the **"I’m a…" view switcher** in the header of every page. It saves the choice in `hfb-audience` and records in `hfb-visited` that the welcome page has been used
- `assets/logo-hfb-original@1x.png`, `@2x.png`, `logo.png`: approved logo. Use as-is and don't edit
- `assets/img/`: web-optimized brand artwork (crystal lotus symbol, cosmic lotus, wordmark banner)
- `play-together/`: the bilingual games (Choice & Reward, Feelings, Match It). Each game folder is self-contained
- `play-together/shared/egg.js` is the shared **surprise egg** shown after every 5 stars
- Keep `CNAME`, `google646d6f24339df9d9.html`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `favicon.png`, `manifest.webmanifest`

## Editing
Every page shares the same header and footer. They are generated by `site-tools/build_site.py` (kept outside the repo, in `/workspace/hfb/site-tools/`). If you edit the nav by hand, change it on every page.
Bump `?v=` on `site.css` and `site.js` after you edit them, to bust caches. The current version is `2026.10.7`.
English pages contain no Persian text. The games have their own FA/EN toggle.
