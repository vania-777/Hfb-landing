Platform · Planned

A social & learning platform for neurodiversity

A calm, multilingual space where neurodivergent people, families, and professionals can connect, learn, and get support, with AI helping human connection instead of replacing it.

Status: in design. The platform below is planned. None of these features are live yet. Our first live tool is the Choice & Reward game.

The first version we plan to build

Four main areas, designed mobile-first for iPhone.

Planned

Polyglot Core: Listen & Translate

One accessibility layer for real communication across languages and processing styles, not only machine translation.

  • Translation in posts, comments, and messages (Persian ↔ English first)
  • Read-Aloud with natural voices
  • Simplify dense text, or expand short text
  • Tone support to help read and soften messages (assistive, not diagnostic)
Planned

Social Spaces

A calmer, more structured alternative to noisy social media.

  • Parents & Caregivers
  • Neurodivergent adults
  • Professionals, therapists & educators
  • Topic rooms, threads, and reactions
Planned

Private Messages

One-to-one and group conversations with translation and Read-Aloud built in.

  • Reporting, blocking, and moderation support
  • Clear consent and privacy controls
Planned

Knowledge Hub

Curated, evidence-informed information instead of random internet advice.

  • A small first library of about 20 resources
  • Autism and ADHD basics, sensory profiles, executive function, self-advocacy, and more
  • Summaries and simpler versions

Also planned

Planned

Expert Connect

Humans, not just algorithms: Q&A, workshops, and referrals with real professionals. AI does not replace professional judgment.

Planned

Reflection Journal

A low-overload space to capture thoughts and notice patterns.

Planned

Private Circles

Smaller private groups next to the public topic spaces.

Design principles

  • Low sensory load and predictable navigation
  • You choose how much is shown and how complex the wording is
  • Personalization you control. Neurodivergent people are not one profile.
  • Community before scale, and clarity over persuasion

Want to follow along?

We are looking for parents, neurodivergent adults, and professionals who would like to share feedback as we design.
