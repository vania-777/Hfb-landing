Choice & Reward

A calm, bilingual (Persian and English) choice-making game for an adult and a child to play together. The child picks one of 2–4 large picture cards, hears what they chose, and earns a star. Five stars bring a gentle celebration.

  • Runs in the browser: no account, no download
  • Settings stay on your device
  • Gentle motion that you can turn off

Feelings

A calm, bilingual (Persian and English) emotion-recognition game. The child sees one friendly cartoon face and picks the matching feeling word: happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, or calm. Right answers earn a star. Wrong answers get no buzzer, just a gentle look at the right one.

  • Hand-drawn cartoon faces with varied skin tones, no photos
  • 2 to 4 choices, and an errorless-learning prompt you can turn on
  • Recorded read-aloud in Persian and English

Match It: What goes together?

A calm, bilingual (Persian and English) drag-to-match game. The child drags the item that goes with a picture: water to a glass, a key to a lock, a bone to a dog. A right match plays a small animation with a sound, then a star. A wrong one just slides gently back.

  • Drag with a finger or a mouse, or tap and tap
  • 12 hand-drawn pairs, each with its own little animation
  • Three levels, recorded read-aloud, and motion you can turn off

How we design

Calm by design

Low sensory load

Predictable navigation, clear hierarchy, little clutter, and no flashing or busy motion.

Privacy first

Collect as little as possible, explain it clearly, and never sell sensitive data.

Humans, not just algorithms

Community, lived experience, and real professionals come first. AI is a helper.
