Choice & Reward: quick guide

  • Open: the game works in any modern browser, on a phone, tablet, or computer.
  • Language: the EN / FA button switches between English and Persian.
  • Adult panel (⚙️): choose 2–4 cards, categories, sound, and motion, or reset stars.
  • Keyboard: Tab, Enter, and Space work everywhere. Keys 1–4 pick a card. Esc closes panels.
  • Read-aloud: uses the voices built into your browser. Some browsers have no Persian voice. The text always stays on screen.
  • Together mode: share your screen with computer audio on a video call.
