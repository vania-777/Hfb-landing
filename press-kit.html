About HFB, in brief

High Functioning Brains (HFB) builds calm, accessible tools for neurodivergent people (ADHD, autism), their families, and professionals. HFB was founded in January 2025 by Vania Zarafshani Sarabi. Its first live tool is Choice & Reward, a bilingual Persian/English game for an adult and child to play together.

Tagline: Neurodiversity. Empowered by Unity.
Line: Empowering minds through science and compassion.
