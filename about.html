About HFB

Empowering minds through science and compassion

High Functioning Brains (HFB) makes calm, accessible tools for neurodivergent people (ADHD, autism) and the families and professionals around them.

Our story

HFB was founded in January 2025 by Vania Zarafshani Sarabi, Founder & Product Developer. It grew where science, empathy, and the lived experience of neurodiversity meet.

We ask how neuroscience, psychology, and human-centered design can empower neurodivergent minds, not “fix” them.

What we believe

  • Privacy first. Your data, your control.
  • Evidence-informed. Guided by research and by lived experience.
  • Inclusion. Tools that respect neurodivergent patterns and strengths.
  • Calm. Low noise, clear words, and no pressure.

Where HFB comes from

  1. 🜂 Inspiration

    Personal journeys and questions worth asking.

  2. 🜃 Research

    Neuroscience, psychology, and education.

  3. 🜁 Design

    Simple, calm, and accessible.

  4. 🜄 Community

    Built with and for neurodivergent people and their families.
