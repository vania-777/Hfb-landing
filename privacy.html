Privacy

Privacy

Short version: this website does not track you.

This website

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies.
  • No forms. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.
  • Your theme choice (dark, dim, or light) is saved in your browser’s local storage, on your device only.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Choice & Reward game

The game has no account and sends nothing to us. Its settings, language, and stars are saved only in your browser’s local storage. You can clear them at any time in the adult panel or in your browser settings.

Future products

For the platform we are designing, we commit to collecting as little as possible, using clear opt-in consent, and never selling sensitive data. We will publish a full privacy policy before anything launches.
