Play together

Free in the browser. No account, no download. Settings stay on your device.

    Choice-making

    Choice & Reward

    The child picks one of 2–4 large picture cards, hears what they chose, and earns a star for the team.

    Play Choice & Reward
  • 😊😢

    Emotion recognition

    Feelings

    The child sees a friendly cartoon face or a little story and picks the matching feeling word.

    Play Feelings
  • 🔒🔑

    Matching

    Match It: What goes together?

    The child drags the item that goes with a picture: water to a glass, a key to a lock. Each match plays a small animation.

    Play Match It
  • 🔥❄️

    Concepts

    Opposites: Hot or cold?

    Picture-only concept pairs for children who don’t read yet: the child sees steam rising from a mug and drags the hot card onto it, sorts pictures into two boxes, then solves little situations.

    Play Opposites
