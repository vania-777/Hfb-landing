What we’re planning

  • Topic groups

    Group chats for parents and caregivers, neurodivergent adults, and therapists and educators, each with clear topics.

  • Create your own group

    Start a small private group for your family, your clinic, or a few friends, and invite only the people you choose.

  • Translate and read aloud

    Persian and English first, with read-aloud and simpler wording, so language is never a wall.

  • Safety first

    Real moderators, clear rules, and tools to report, block and leave. Privacy settings you control.

  • Calm by design

    Quiet notifications, no ads, no endless feeds, and no pressure to reply right away.

  • Humans first

    Lived experience and real professionals come first. AI only helps with things like translation.

Draft community guidelines

A first draft. We will shape them together with the people who join.

  1. Kindness

    Be patient and kind. Every brain works differently, and every family’s path is different.

  2. Privacy

    Share only what you are comfortable with. Never share anyone else’s story, photos or details without permission.

  3. Respect

    Respect people’s words for themselves, such as “autistic person” or “person with autism”.

  4. Care

    Share experience, not diagnoses. Group chats do not replace professional or medical advice.

  5. Safety

    Report anything that feels unsafe. Moderators will review it.
